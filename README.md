**Experimental infrastructure for autonomous business execution**

Additional engineering includes capability expiration, connector receipts, bounded canary execution, webhook replay checks, independent QA, and ledger checkpoints.

**My role:** Project initiator and product owner, defining the autonomy goals, requirements, and evidence standards.

**Status:** Experimental implementation; source is not published here. Fixture and simulation results are distinct from production evidence. Real customer payment and autonomous delivery are not established by the reviewed implementation record.

**Status:** Specification and starter workspace. A functioning physical robot, completed autonomy stack, and measured runtime have not been demonstrated.
